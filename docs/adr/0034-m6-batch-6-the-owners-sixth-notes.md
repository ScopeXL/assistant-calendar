# ADR 0034: The owner's sixth notes (M6, batch 6)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-16

## Context

- Sunroom 0.11.0 went on the owner's wall and phones, and their sixth set of notes was the widest
  yet: the words for devices and screens; setup's password, name and theme; About and the
  Location note; "Return to the calendar after: Never" ignored by Settings; tips on by default;
  four more colors; stars for several children; the Who picker's padding; a Support Sunroom page;
  people assigned from an event's title and chips in several colors; reminders that don't come
  back and a default reminder; errors surfaced in one place; no proxy setting; a refresh that
  shows the new version; the Backup page rebuilt, and restore from a file. `docs/M6-batch-6.md`
  turns them into tasks, answers the owner's questions and records every decision; this ADR keeps
  the ones that change an earlier decision or that a later session would otherwise have to
  rediscover (PLAN §15, M6 → 0.12.0). It was started with the batch's first task and gained a row
  with each task after.

## Decision

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Devices and screens | **A device is anything signed in with the household password** (a phone, tablet or computer); **a screen is a wall screen paired with a code** ("paired screens", "this screen"; "Kitchen screen" stays as the default name a new one gets). The app no longer says "phone or computer" for what any device can do, and a paired screen shows **nothing it can't do and no line saying where to do it**: the owner's rule is that whoever can see a setting can use it. So Backup on a screen keeps the backups and Back Up Now and drops its three "from a phone or computer" lines (and the export group, which had only that line there); a person's sheet on a screen shows the photo row only to remove a photo; Start Over stays off the screens (a child can reach them, ADR 0029). Where the screen is showing something that genuinely needs a device (Google's own sign-in page, a helper's key file, adding photos), it keeps the QR code that opens it on one, worded "from a device"; an account that Google signed out now shows that code ("Sign in again from a device:") where it had a sentence. Devices' second group is **Signed-in Devices** (a page named Devices can't hold a group named Devices); the server's refusals say "a signed-in device" and "a paired screen". Two places keep "phone", because they are about one: setup, before any screen exists, and installing the app. Two new tips say what signed-in devices can do that a screen can't (one planned tip was 96 characters, over the list's 90, and was shortened) |
| The household password | **8 characters or more** (it was 12): `MIN_PASSWORD_LENGTH`, `APP_PASSWORD`'s check, the seed's bound and the wizard. The hint still says "A few words together work well.": a passphrase is what keeps it strong, and the household password is the one thing a stranger on the network could guess at (sign-in stays rate-limited per address and in all, PLAN §12.1). The owner asked for 8 |
| Setup's household step | The name **starts as Home**, with the chips Home · Family · Our home (Home first; "The family" became Family). **Theme** Light / Dark / Auto is on this step, after the week's first day, with one line saying what Auto does; Auto by default. Not a step of its own (seven steps for one three-way choice) and not Where's home (which skips itself with the weather off). The choice shows on the page at once (`lib/theme.previewTheme`: the wizard runs in the phone's shell with no household yet, so it puts the appearance on itself and remembers it for the next page's first paint; Auto follows the device there, as it will after setup) and goes to the server with `POST /api/setup` (`SetupIn.theme`, `auto` by default). The password step says "devices" and "Paired screens don't need it."; You're Set says "a screen on the wall" |
| About's words | The row that said "This device uses" is **URL**; the group is **Updates**, its switch **Check for updates daily**, its quiet line "This is the latest version (checked at …)." and the server's refusal "Turn on Check for updates daily first."; The Small Print keeps the licence and "Your data stays on your server…" and gains "Weather by Open-Meteo." (UX §4 had promised it; only the forecast's sheet said it); "Not affiliated with Google or Apple." went. **The author's line ("Made by …") is not built yet**: the private-terms scan treats the owner's name as private everywhere but LICENSE's copyright line, and the plan leaves it to the owner to choose how it clears (extend the scan's exemption to that one About line, take the name off the private-terms list, or drop the line); a session never allowlists a hit on its own (CLAUDE.md, hard rule 1) |
| Return to the calendar after | It governs **every room, Settings included** (2, 5 or 10 minutes, or Never). The owner set Never and was sent home after 2 minutes: Settings had its own hard-coded rule (lock the parent grant **and go home** after 2 idle minutes), whatever the setting said. Now the grant still locks after 2 idle minutes (looking never needed it; a change asks the PIN again), and the page leaves with the other rooms, after the household's minutes, never with Never. The other place that sent the wall home, a screensaver woken after 30 minutes, goes home only when the setting is a number. The board's own return to now after 2 minutes is unchanged (ADR 0031) |
| The PIN pad on a page left open | **On the wall the PIN pad rises for a refusal only within 30 seconds of a touch** (`lib/parent.askPinOnlyWhen`, set by the display shell from `lib/idle`). A Settings page can now stay open after its grant locks, and some of its reads are a parent's (Devices, Backup, About's diagnostics, the accounts): a live update or a reconnect refreshes them, the server refuses, and the pad would have come up for nobody. Such a refresh now just fails quietly (the page keeps what it showed, and says "Only a parent can do that. Enter the parent PIN." where it shows errors); a tap still asks as ever. Phones never set a rule: a request there follows a tap. Not in the plan; found while building the return |
| Tips | `show_tips` is **on by default**, and one migration turns it on once for every household (the owner's wish; a household can turn it off again). The list's file (`features/tips/tips.ts`) says at its top how to add or change a tip and what `tips.test.ts` checks |
| Four more colors | **Copper "Orange", bark "Brown", slate "Gray" and lime "Lime"**, appended after rose so nobody's color and no picker's order moves (`next_color` still hands a new person the first free one, and a synced calendar's pick order puts them last). Light `#A54C00` `#7D5B40` `#50667C` `#637200`, dark `#CA7D44` `#AE8A6D` `#7B95B0` `#8B9D26`: OKLCH at the eight's lightness, and every check in the token test passes as planned, with no nudge (white on the light solids 5:1 or more, the solids 4.5:1 on every wall tint and the surface, ink on the tints, the dark solids as text on the dark surface). Beyond an orange, only muted colors are far enough from the eight. Twelve everywhere: the backend's lists and `PersonColor`, `lib/colors`, the tokens, both pickers. The Add panel's thirteen dots wrap to two lines on the wall; dots aren't chips, so that's accepted. This changes ADR 0013's eight and 0029's picker. **C1 fixed with it:** choosing "The person's color" for an event that had its own now clears it (`clear_color`, at every scope) |
| Stars | **Several children at once**: `POST /api/chores/points/adjust` takes `member_ids` (1 to 20, deduplicated, each an active member, else 422 `unknown_member` and nothing written), one ledger row each in one transaction, and answers `PointsOut` (their stars, in the order asked; the plan said a bare list, but an object matches `GET /points` and can grow). **Custom…** is an opener chip after the presets: Give or Take and a stepper of 1 to 100 stars, starting at the chosen preset's size and direction. The button says Give Stars or Take Stars by the sign; the toast names everyone ("Took 3 stars from Mia and Leo": `chores/words.starsToast`); the children stay picked after, the reason clears |
| The Who picker's ring | A cell is `py-2` on the wall (8 px above the avatar and under the name, matching the 8 px at its sides around a 64 px avatar in an 80 px cell) and stays `py-1` on phones, whose 56 px cells around 48 px avatars already have 4 px every way. The owner found the ring hugging the avatar's top and the name's foot. The row is 8 px taller on the wall; nothing measures it, and the five-people row and the wall's tap-target journey still pass. The other "chosen person" looks (chips with an avatar, the Filter menu, a phone's Show row) were roomier already and didn't change |
| Support Sunroom | A Settings page before About: what Sunroom is (free, open source, not for profit) and the owner's ways to support it, from one constants file the owner fills in (`features/settings/support.ts`: the repository, Patreon, donation addresses; an empty value hides its row, and the group with nothing set is its note alone). An address has a QR code (wallets scan these) and **Copy** on a device, through `lib/copy` (it copies over plain http too, so Copy shows on every device, not only in a secure context as planned); a paired screen shows no Copy and no link, only codes. **Send Feedback** can't post to GitHub from the app (the CSP's `connect-src 'self'`, and no token in a public image), so it builds GitHub's new-issue link with the kind as the title's prefix, the words and "Sunroom 0.12.0, Docker" (version and install from diagnostics), at most 2,000 characters (the words are cut, by halving, never the title or the version) and opens it in a new tab with **Open GitHub** (`ui/ButtonLink`, an `<a>` that looks like a button and has no address until there's a title). The person signs in to GitHub there and taps Submit. On a paired screen: no box for the words (the wall's keyboard types into one-line fields only, and the link carries the title only, so its QR code stays readable), a QR code of the link, and Done. **The one emoji in the app's own copy** is the heart beside the page's heading, the owner's choice, hidden from screen readers (`Group`'s new `mark`); UX §2 records the exception |

## Consequences

- No schema change from the words.
- UX §2 has a "Devices and screens" paragraph; PLAN §2's glossary names devices and paired
  screens.
- New in the API: `theme` in `POST /api/setup` (`SetupIn`, `auto` by default); the password's
  least length is 8 everywhere it's checked. New in the frontend: `lib/theme.previewTheme`.
- Migration `202610161454_household_tips_on_by_default`: `household.show_tips` gets server
  default true and is set to true for every household, once. Tested from the 0.11.0 fixture (a
  household with tips off comes out with them on).
