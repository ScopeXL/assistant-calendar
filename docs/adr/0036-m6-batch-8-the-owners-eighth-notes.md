# ADR 0036: The owner's eighth notes (M6, batch 8)

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-21

## Context

- Sunroom 0.13.0 went on the owner's wall and phones, and their notes of 2026-10-21 were four:
  the People view's arrows don't change the day; lists and tasks can be made but hardly managed
  (nothing on an item or a task says it can be changed or removed, a list goes only through
  Change List, and a task due next month is nowhere to be seen); Rolling week should be a switch
  on the Week view itself; and the screensaver should draw on an Immich photo server, filtered by
  people, albums or tags, as the first of several photo libraries. `docs/M6-batch-8.md` turns
  them into tasks, answers the owner's questions and records every decision; this ADR keeps the
  ones that change an earlier decision or that a later session would otherwise have to
  rediscover (PLAN §15, M6 → 0.14.0). It was started with the batch's first task and gained a row
  with each task after.

## Decision

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| People's day | **The arrows step the shown day on People as on Day** (`displayState.day`, shared by the two views, so paging one and switching to the other keeps the day): **Previous day**, **Next day**, and **Today** as the quick jump, disabled on today; the idle return already puts `day` back. The header's arrows were one handler for every view, and People had no branch, so its taps moved the Week's hidden `weekOffset` (the next visit to Week opened weeks ahead) while the page turn slid the columns back to the same day. **The day is written over the columns on every day**, one `DayCaption` shared with the Day view ("Thursday, October 8", with "today" beside it on today); the owner asked for it when not today, and always is one look with the Day view. People's h1 stays "People", so there the caption is read by a screen reader (the Day view's h1 already names its day, and hides its caption). The events, the Free day pill, the past events' dimming (today only) and the plugins' column under each person follow the day; **a person's tasks have working boxes on today only**: another day's rows say done (a check mark and "Done by Mia · 7:42 AM") or the due line in words, and the title still opens the task (the API would take a completion on any day the rule makes; the column, not the API, keeps a child from doing tomorrow's tasks for stars). **The Today view has no arrows and no quick jump** (`BoardControls`'s `paging`): it is always today and the six days after, and its arrows paged nothing |
| Rolling week, per screen | **This screen's own choice** (`displayState.rollingWeek: boolean \| null`, null meaning the household's), by ADR 0031's way: remembered across reloads, kept through the idle return like the layout, and **a change of Settings → Display → Rolling week wins** (`HouseholdChoices.rollingWeek`), so the household's switch still sets every screen. A switch in the header that PATCHed the household setting was rejected: that route is parent-only, so a child's tap would raise the PIN pad, and with no PIN one tap would change every screen and phone. The control is **one round icon cell, Rolling week** (`ui/IconToggle`, lucide's `CalendarRange`), 8 px after Agenda · Hours, on the Week view only (laptops in the display shell too; phones never), pressed when on. A view kept by 0.13.0 has no such key and still comes back (`remembered()` reads a missing key as null; a new required field would have wiped every screen's memory once). **The header's room**, measured at 1920 Standard with both panels open: on the widest date and time (Wed, Sep 30, 12:30 PM, a two-digit temperature) one row with nobody filtered (44 px to spare) or one person (4 px), a second row with two or more (the filter clears after 2 idle minutes); on an ordinary morning (Wed, Oct 7, 9:40 AM) one row up to three people (8 px), two rows with four. The plan expected the wrap from three people on the widest dates; it comes at two. That is the header's designed overflow ("wraps rather than clips", UX §3), the two-row header Large, Extra large, laptops and portrait always have; folding Agenda · Hours into a menu, dropping the chosen view's word or floating a pill on the board were each worse. 16 px between the layout and the cell (its own group) would have cost the one-person case |
| Rows that open | **Every row that opens something ends in a chevron**, the mark Settings' rows already carry (`ui/ListRow`): an item's row on the wall and on phones, its words and meta one button with the chevron at its end, so the row reads as the button it always was (tapping an item's words already opened its sheet, and nothing said so). The sheets are kept: an item's (What, Who, Day, **Remove** with Undo, Save Changes) as it was. A list's doors stay **Change List** and the tile's long press (a tip says so); Change List's **Remove** spans the panel above the pinned Save Changes, so it's found without looking. Not chosen: a trash or pencil icon on every row (a second target inside a row that is already a button), a long press on rows (nothing shows it exists), a confirmation on Remove (Undo and Recently Removed cover it, UX §1) |
| Swipe | **Phones only**: a row swiped to the left shows **Remove** (`ui/SwipeRow`: `motion`'s drag on the row's content under the existing `MotionConfig`, a `danger`-filled button 6rem wide behind it), a shortcut for the sheet's Remove, never the only way; it does exactly what the sheet's does, toast and Undo. One row is open at a time, a press anywhere else closes it, and an open row's first tap only closes it; a drag under 8 px is still a tap, and the click after a swipe is swallowed. Past half the button or a flick left opens it (`swipeSettles`). Hidden, the button is `inert`, `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order, so a screen reader meets the sheet's Remove alone. On the list of lists, Remove on a list with anything on it opens Change List at its asking step ("Groceries still has 12 things on it." · Remove · Keep); an empty list goes at once with Undo. **The wall never swipes rows**: its columns scroll sideways under a finger, and a wet finger on the kitchen screen shouldn't find a Remove by accident. A link in a swiped row would start the browser's own drag, so the row cancels `dragstart`. **A swipe is offered only where its Remove can work**: a phone has no PIN pad (it's mounted on the wall only), so a parent-only refusal there would wait for one for ever; a list swipes on a parent's phone or for the list's own maker (as the server allows), an item on any phone (a child's refusal for someone else's item is a toast) |
| A task's sheet | Opened from a box (Today, People) or from All Tasks, it says **the task in words** under its title (`ruleLine`: the rule, the next day, by when, its stars, whose), then **Change**, which on the wall **asks the PIN before the form opens** (as an event's Change does; the save asked it only after the form was filled in), **Skip Today** only while the task repeats and is due today (it skips today, whichever day's box opened it), and **Remove** with Undo. Every task row ends in a chevron, as an item's does; a phone swipes it for Remove **only where Remove can work**: on a device with a parent's rights (a parent's phone, or one holding the PIN's grant). A child's phone gets no swipe on tasks, since removing one is a parent's and a phone has no PIN pad (the plan expected "the server's plain refusal as today"; on a phone with a PIN set, `asParent` would wait for the pad for ever) |
| All Tasks | A third choice in the Tasks room's **Show** control (wall and phone): **Today · This Week · All Tasks**. A column per person a task names, in household order, then Anyone (`taskColumns`: a fixed task under each of its people, a turn-taking one under each person in its turns, an anyone task under Anyone), headed "Mia · 4 tasks"; every task soonest first, its title over its rule line ("Every week on Sat · Sat, Nov 14 · by 5:00 PM · ★2", "Once · Tomorrow", "Every day · Today · Mia and Leo take turns"), a chevron, **no box** (a box completes, and that belongs to Today), a tap opening the task. On a phone, sections as Today's (the phone's person first and open, and Anyone's). For it, every task gains **`next_due`**, computed by `domain.chores.next_due`: a repeating task's first day from today (skipped days left out; None when the rule has ended or makes nothing within a year), a one-off's day while that's today or later, today while it's carried over undone, and None once done or skipped; `GET /api/chores` sorts by it (None last), then title. A task with none reads **"Finished"** in its line (the plan had nothing there; a row with no day looked broken). **The board's columns** count a person with a task still ahead, or a box on the days shown: a one-off done late keeps its column on the day it was stamped, and the week keeps the columns its boxes had; a household whose tasks are all finished sees the room's empty state |
| Finished tasks | **A finished task leaves by itself**, as a past countdown does (ADR 0026): in the hourly job (`chores:prune` → `tidy`), an active task with no next day, untouched for 24 hours, whose last day (a one-off's day; a repeat's by its UNTIL or COUNT, `recurrence.last_day`) is before today, gets `deleted_at` and shows in Recently Removed. Its completions, stars and streaks stay: they're history. **Put Back** sets `updated_at`, so it stays a day and then leaves again unless its day changed. A paused task (`active` false) is left alone; a rule that never ends is never tidied, even with nothing within a year (a February 29). Not chosen: a "Finished" group kept for ever (the family asked to see what's coming) |
| The home-network tick | **A parent's tick writes its host into the network allowlist** (`ctx.network.allow`, a new context facade over `household.service.allow_network`; the plugin never touches the table), **before the first check**, since the guard wants both the tick and the row, and the row is taken back if that check fails. The rule (PLAN §12.6) is unchanged, its words are: the guard's sentence and Add an Account's hints stopped pointing at a Settings → Network page that never existed, so before this a home Nextcloud or Immich couldn't be allowed from any screen. **Each feature keeps its own rows** ("Calendars & Accounts", "Photos & Screensaver" as the label, the plan's per-account label would have broken on a rename), added unless that feature's row for the host is there; `disallow` takes out only that feature's rows, once none of its live accounts or sources reaches the host with the tick, so two features (or two accounts) on one server never cut each other off, and a row added by hand stays. The add routes are a parent's, so the tick is too |
| The guard | `X-Api-Key` joins the credential headers that never follow a redirect to another server (a library's key would have gone wherever a redirect pointed); the log redactor masks `api-key`, `api_key` and `apikey` (it matched only `api_key`, not the header) |
| Photo libraries | **Copies on the volume** through core's store (ADR 0009), `source_key = "source:<id>"`, as the inbox's are: the page's content-security policy shows images from Sunroom's own address only, and the key must never reach a browser. What's brought in is the library's **preview** (about 1440 px, re-encoded as WebP by the store), dated from the library's record (a preview has no EXIF: `PhotoStore.ingest` takes a `taken_at`), at most **Keep up to** 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 (500 by default), newest first. A library's photos can be hidden from the screensaver, not removed one by one (the store's dedupe would bring them back); removing the library removes them, softly, with its mapping and its key. A library switched off keeps its photos and the manifest shows none of them. **Immich first of many**: the library code is one small interface (`sources/base.Library`: `check`, `choices`, `find`, `fetch`, `thumb`) behind a registry keyed by `kind`; the API says which kinds exist and what each asks for (`GET library-kinds`), and everything after a fetch knows nothing of Immich. On the test server, an Immich at `http://immich.test` with the key `sample-key` is the pretend one (`sources/fake.py`), so the screens and the e2e tests walk the real path with nothing on the network |
| The picks | People, albums and tags, by the library's ids; **any pick matches**; nothing picked means every photo. Immich's own search means "all of these" for several people, so Sunroom asks once per pick and merges. The choices are fetched when the library is connected and cached (`options_json`), fetched again when the sheet asks for them and they're over an hour old (a failure keeps the cache) and on Refresh Choices (a failure says so); the people offered are the named ones, the unnamed counted. The picks save as they change, and a change of picks, Keep up to or the switch starts a pass |
| Immich's API | Checked against Immich's OpenAPI spec at **v2.7.5** (the newest 2.x) and **v3.3.4** (the newest release, published the day before this batch): 3.x is current, so the client is "Immich 2.0 and newer", and an older server is refused with a plain line ("Sunroom works with Immich 2.0 and newer; this one is 1.106."). Smallest fixes to the plan's names: the version from `GET /api/server/version` (no permission; `/server/about` needs `server.about`); **an album's photos through the search's `albumIds`** (since 3.0, `GET /api/albums/{id}` carries no `assets`); a photo's own `width` and `height`; `visibility: "timeline"` and `withDeleted: false` for archived and trashed photos; the library's photo count from `GET /api/assets/statistics?visibility=timeline` (the search's `total` counts one page). Since 3.2.0 the search's flat filters and `page` are deprecated for a `filter` object and a `cursor`; they still answer and are all 2.x knows, so they're used, and an Immich that drops them needs the new form (a follow-up) |
| A pass | `libraries.run_pass`: the library's picked photos (newest first, up to Keep up to), the mapping kept in step (`photo_source_items`: a photo no longer picked dropped and its copy removed softly; one picked again restored without a fetch while core still has it, a photo hidden from the screensaver staying hidden), then **at most 200 fetches**, each encoded before a short write transaction of its own, `photos.changed` after every 20; a pass with work left starts the next after 2 seconds, so a first fill of 500 is done in minutes while the wall shows the first ones. A fetch that fails is tried on the next two passes, then left out (`fetch_failures`, a column the plan didn't list). Passes run one at a time per library, **each in a task of its own**: the 30-minute job (`screensaver:library-sync`) only starts them, so a slow library never holds up the inbox, and a failing one stays inside (a spawned task that raised would stop the plugin). A refused key leaves the library `needs_reconnect` ("Immich needs its key again." in Needs Attention), a server that doesn't answer `error` ("Immich hasn't answered. Showing the photos it had."); its photos keep showing. A key wiped by a new secret key: boot sets `needs_reconnect` (the table has a `status` now), and the next pass says "Sunroom can't read this library's saved key any more. Connect it again." Check Now is the library's own `POST libraries/{id}/scan` (it starts a pass); the inbox's `sources/{id}/scan` stays the inbox's, and `GET sources` lists only the inbox |
| Where the screens are | Settings → **Photos & Screensaver** gains **Photo Libraries** after the photos folder: a line ("Sunroom can show photos kept on a photo server. Immich works today."), a row per library (its name, how it's doing in red when a parent can fix it, its count, a chevron) and **Add a Library**; **one panel** for connecting and for a library (`LibraryPanel`): connected, it turns into the library's own page, so the family picks at once. The picks are faces (the library's own face thumbnails, proxied through Sunroom so the key never reaches a browser; the initial in a neutral circle when there's none), album and tag chips with counts (chosen first; a **Find an album** field over a dozen), and the sentence (`picksSentence`), which follows every tap; each tap saves (the quiet "Saved"). The home-network switch starts ticked for an address that looks like home (`lib/address.isHomeAddress`: localhost, a private IP, a name with no dots or ending in .local, .lan, .home or .internal) and is always the family's. **Remove Library** asks in place, as Change List does, and has no Undo (its key goes with it). The Photos room's viewer and the phone's sheet say "From Immich" for a library's photo (the libraries' names are a parent's to read; elsewhere "From a photo library"), and the phone offers Hide, not Remove; the server refuses too (`DELETE /api/photos/{id}` → 409 `from_library`). Needs Attention needs nothing new: the plugin's problems already name the page |

## Consequences

- No schema change from the People row. New in the frontend: `features/calendar/DayCaption.tsx`
  (the Day and People views), `BoardControls`'s `paging`, `ChoreColumn`'s and `ChoreRow`'s
  `readOnly`; the plugins' person column reads its `day`.
- No schema change from Rolling week. New in the frontend: `ui/IconToggle`; `DisplayState`'s
  `rollingWeek` and `HouseholdChoices`' `rollingWeek`; a tip ("rolling").
- No schema change from the rows. New in the frontend: `ui/SwipeRow` (`swipeSettles`, `REVEAL`);
  `ChangeListPanel`'s `asking`; two tips ("item-sheet", "list-hold").
- No schema change from the tasks. New in the API: `next_due` on `ChoreOut` (every route that
  answers a task); `GET /api/chores` soonest first. New backend: `domain.chores.next_due` and
  `last_due`, `domain.recurrence.last_day`, `chores.service.next_days` and `tidy` (in the hourly
  prune); `_column_ids` reads the book and the boxes. New frontend: `features/chores/AllTasks.tsx`
  (`AllTasksColumns`, `TaskRuleRow`, `taskColumns`), `words.nextWord` and `ruleLine`; `ChoreSheet`
  takes a task (`{ chore_id, title }`) and is exported; two tips ("all-tasks", "task-sheet").
- No schema change from the tick: the allowlist table is the one M0 made. New in the backend:
  `ctx.network` (`NetworkFacade`), `household.service.allow_network`, `disallow_network` and
  `allow_target` (moved from the router); the sync plugin's add, change, reconnect and
  disconnect write and release their rows. `.env.example` names `SUNROOM_ALLOW_PRIVATE_URLS`.
- **One migration**, `202610211809_screensaver_photo_libraries`: `photo_sources` gains `status`
  (server default `connected`), `options_json` (`{}`) and `options_at`; the new table
  `photo_source_items` (owned by the screensaver plugin, not exported). Tested from the 0.13.0
  fixture (every source connected, nothing cached, no items); the 0.14.0 fixture
  `202610211809.sql` is built (a library needing its key, one photo brought in, one waiting).
- New in the API: the library routes under `/api/screensaver` (`library-kinds`, `libraries`, a
  library's `choices`, `thumb`, `reconnect` and `scan`, and `_test/library` on the test server)
  and their `Library…` shapes. `GET sources` lists the inbox only. New backend:
  `plugins/screensaver/libraries.py`, `sources/{base,immich,fake,registry}.py`;
  `PhotoStore.ingest(taken_at=)`; `service.photo_counts` shared.
- New in the frontend: `features/screensaver/LibraryPanel.tsx`, `words.ts` (`picksSentence`,
  `libraryLine`, `connectedLine`), the library hooks and changes in `data.ts` (`fromLibrary`);
  `lib/address.isHomeAddress`; a tip ("photo-libraries"). New in the API: `PhotoOut.source_key`;
  `DELETE /api/photos/{id}` answers 409 `from_library` for a library's photo.
